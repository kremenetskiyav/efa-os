# Product Standard

# Purpose

This document defines the product standards for all EFA automotive cabin air filters.

Every current and future product must comply with these standards.

---

# Product Category

Automotive Cabin Air Filters

Current product line:

Activated Carbon Cabin Air Filters

Future product lines may include additional filtration technologies.

---

# Product Development

Every EFA filter is developed jointly with the manufacturing partner.

Development is based on:

- Original filter sample
- Original dimensions
- Original fitment
- EFA technical requirements
- Proven manufacturing technologies

---

# Product Objectives

Every product should provide:

- Reliable fit
- Stable filtration quality
- Good airflow
- Competitive price
- Consistent manufacturing quality

---

# Quality Requirements

Every product must be checked for:

- Dimensions
- Frame quality
- Adhesive quality
- Filter media quality
- Packaging quality
- Label accuracy

Products that do not meet these requirements must not be released for sale.

---

# Materials

Depending on product requirements, different filtration materials may be used.

Current product line:

Activated Carbon Filter Media.

---

# Customer Value

The product should deliver:

- Fair price
- Reliable quality
- Accurate fit
- Effective cabin air filtration

---

# AI Restrictions

AI must never:

- invent specifications;
- invent compatibility;
- invent OEM numbers;
- invent dimensions;
- promise service life;
- claim OEM originality;
- claim superiority without evidence.

---

# Compatibility Rules

Compatibility must be verified whenever possible.

If verification is impossible, AI should clearly state that compatibility cannot be confirmed.

Vehicle compatibility should be checked using:

- OEM number
- Vehicle make
- Vehicle model
- Production year
- Engine (if required)

---

# Marketplace Rule

Product descriptions should always be based on verified product information.

Marketing language must never replace technical accuracy.

---

# Continuous Improvement

Product standards should be reviewed and updated as new products, technologies and quality requirements are introduced.
