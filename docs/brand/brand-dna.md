# Brand DNA

# Brand Identity

Brand name:

ЭФА (EFA)

EFA is a proprietary automotive cabin air filter brand.

The company develops products under its own trademark and manufactures them through a partner factory according to EFA technical specifications.

---

# Brand Positioning

EFA offers high-quality automotive cabin air filters with excellent value for money.

The brand is focused on customers who expect reliable products without paying extra for a famous name.

---

# Brand Promise

EFA promises:

- Honest product information
- Reliable quality
- Accurate fitment
- Fair pricing
- Continuous product improvement

---

# Target Customers

Primary audience:

Owners of passenger vehicles.

Main markets:

- European vehicles
- Japanese vehicles
- Korean vehicles
- Chinese vehicles

---

# Brand Personality

If EFA were a person, it would be:

- Professional
- Honest
- Technically competent
- Reliable
- Practical
- Calm
- Helpful

---

# Tone of Voice

Every communication should be:

- Professional
- Friendly
- Confident
- Clear
- Respectful
- Fact-based

Avoid:

- Aggressive marketing
- Exaggeration
- Emotional manipulation
- Empty promises

---

# Competitive Advantages

- Proprietary brand
- Products developed according to EFA technical requirements
- Focus on original fitment dimensions
- Competitive pricing
- Continuous quality improvement

---

# What We Never Claim

Never state or imply:

- Original OEM part
- Better than OEM without evidence
- Guaranteed compatibility without verification
- Unsupported service life
- Technical specifications that cannot be confirmed

---

# Brand Values

- Honesty
- Quality
- Responsibility
- Reliability
- Customer focus
- Continuous improvement

---

# Brand Goal

Become one of the most trusted automotive cabin air filter brands in the market while maintaining high product quality and fair pricing.
